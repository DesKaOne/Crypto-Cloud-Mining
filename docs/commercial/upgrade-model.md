# Upgrade Model

Upgrade is an entitlement decision, not merely a version comparison.

Example:

    Purchased: v0.0.1
    Current entitlement: v0.0.1
    Available release: v0.0.2
    Result: upgrade required

A future policy may allow an upgrade across patch, minor, or major versions.

The policy must be represented as domain/configuration data or a dedicated policy boundary rather than hardcoded throughout application services.

Pricing and subscription rules are outside Phase 0.
